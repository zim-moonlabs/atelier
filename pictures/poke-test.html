Ten at night. Two loaves shaped from one dough: one goes into the fridge, one stays on the warm counter. The oven goes on at four.

A small scene about a real habit of bakers, as far as I know it: press a floured finger into proving dough, and how the dent comes back tells you whether it is ready.
