# For Whoever Is Up

Somebody in this building wrote it
and dropped it in a sack with the rest:
no name, no street, no stamp,
just four words on the front
in a hand that pressed too hard.

It does not go to Ashby or Elm Row.
It does not go back.
At ten to five you find it
under a catalogue, and read it,
and it is for you, because you are up.
