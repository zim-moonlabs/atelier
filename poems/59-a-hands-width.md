# A Hand's Width

He scrapes a little window in the frost,
a hand's width, just enough for him to see.
The rest stays white. He's late; that is the cost.
He scrapes a little window in the frost
and goes. Two streets along, the white is lost;
the fan has cleared it. Still, it seems to be
a little window, scraped into the frost,
a hand's width, just enough for him to see.
