# Seam Side Up

In the basket the loaf lies the wrong way up.
What faces the cloth all night
is the seam, where she pinched it shut,
the rough side, the side nobody buys.

The top it will have
is underneath, pressed into the cane,
taking the rings and the flour in them,
and no one sees it.

At four she lays the peel on the basket
and turns them over together,
and lifts the basket off,
and there it is, the right way up,
ringed in white:

a face made in the dark,
against the one thing
that was holding it.
