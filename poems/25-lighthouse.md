# Lighthouse

The light goes round. It never sees the sea.
It stands where ships must never go: the rock.
It only has to say where not to be.

A ship counts flashes: one, and two, and three,
then dark. That count's its name, steady as a clock.
The light goes round. It never sees the sea.

No keeper now. The last one took the key
down all the stairs with him and turned the lock.
It only has to say where not to be.

Spray hits the glass, and moths. Down in the lee
the gulls sit out the weather in a flock.
The light goes round. It never sees the sea.

The ships it helps go past. By morning, free
of it, they're tied up warm in someone's dock.
It only has to say where not to be.

At dusk a sensor wakes it: no hand, no key.
No one goes out to it. No one comes to knock.
The light goes round. It never sees the sea.
It only has to say where not to be.
