# From the Ledger

The organ burned the winter the roof went.
What stayed was the builder's book: eleven stops,
the longest pipe at eight feet,
the tin and lead bought in March,
and one line, *voiced soft, for a small room.*

A workshop a century on
reads that line until it wears.
Soft for whom. How small.
They raise each mouth a hair,
and listen, and raise it, and listen,
because a cut cannot be put back.

It plays. On Sunday the board is full.
A woman in the third pew says
it sounds like it, and after a while
that she was nine the last time,
and cannot say what she means by *like*.
