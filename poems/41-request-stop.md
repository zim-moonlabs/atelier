# Request Stop

Someone presses the red button
and the whole bus is told:
one ding, and STOPPING lit at the front
in letters for everyone.

Nobody asks whose hand.
The driver pulls in without turning round,
and all of them lean with the braking
as if they had all asked.

The doors fold. One coat goes out into the rain.
The sign goes dark,
ready to say it again for someone else.
