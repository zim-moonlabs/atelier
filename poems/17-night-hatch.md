# Night Hatch

The pharmacy is closed, but its bell is not. You press it and wait under the green cross, which hums.

A light comes on at the back, far in, past the shelves of things for coughs and feet. Then slippers. Then a small door at the height of a letterbox opens in the big door, and a face is framed in it, half asleep and entirely awake.

You say the name of the thing. You say it again, because it is long. A hand takes the paper through the hatch, the hatch closes, and you stand alone with the cross for the length of one drawer being opened somewhere inside.

It comes back in a paper bag folded twice at the top. The change comes back in a dish. *Twice a day, with food.* The small door shuts. The light at the back goes out.

Walking home you carry the bag the way you would carry something warm, though it is not warm, and up the street, in the one lit window, someone is waiting for it.
