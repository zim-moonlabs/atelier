# Ice on the Birdbath

The birdbath froze last night into a lid.
At eight a blackbird comes to drink, and lands
on a floor where water was. Whatever he did
here yesterday, today he only stands,

then taps the grey, and tilts his head, and taps,
as though the ice might answer something new.
Two sparrows try the rim and leave. A gap.
The yard goes still. The sun is working through

the fence: a slat of light, and then a slat,
and where it falls, the stone goes dark and wet,
a ring of water one beak wide, and that
is all the morning has to offer yet.

He steps up off the middle to the rim
and drinks the edge of what was holding him.
