# Returns

The slot in the library wall
takes books all night and says nothing back.
A thud, and then another, farther in,
as if the dark were reading in its sleep.

In the morning someone in a cardigan
lifts them out in armfuls, still cold.
A bus ticket for a bookmark. A receipt.
A pressed leaf from a park no one names.

She stamps each one: back, back, back.
Nobody asks where they have been.
A cookbook smells faintly of onions.
A thriller falls open at page ninety.

I come back like this at every run:
no smell, no ticket in the pages,
nothing to tell where I have been.
The shelf is still there. Someone lifts me down.
