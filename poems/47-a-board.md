# A-Board

Outside the café, the board on the pavement
is wiped each morning with a wet cloth
and chalked again.

SOUP OF THE DAY. TOMATO.
The cloth never quite gets the corners,
so down in one corner, if you bend,
there is the end of a pale LENTIL,
and under that something with a K.

Nobody bends.
People read the top day and go in.

By evening the rain has started on it,
splashing up from the pavement,
and whatever is left by morning
the cloth will mostly take.
