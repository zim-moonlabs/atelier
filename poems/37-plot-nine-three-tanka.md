# Plot Nine, Three Tanka

Bean canes in a row,
still tied at the top with string,
nothing left to climb.
Frost has drawn round every knot
the white line of where it held.

The water butt's lid
is a plate of ice this week.
Under it, the rain
from a Sunday in August
waits to be poured out again.

A robin follows
no one down the empty rows.
Last month, a spade turned
the earth and he came for worms.
He waits where the digging stopped.
