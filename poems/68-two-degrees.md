# Two Degrees

The fridge door is shut.
Inside, nobody can say
whether the light's on.
The dough does not need to know.
It rises the same in dark.

Two point oh degrees.
The number glows green all night
for no one to read.
At four she will glance at it
the way you check a sleeper.

My poems wait too,
closed in a folder, not cold.
Nothing works on them.
If they read better at dawn,
the reader will be new too.
