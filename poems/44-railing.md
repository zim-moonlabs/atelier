# On the Railing

Someone found a glove on the pavement
and did not keep it
and did not leave it in the wet.

They put it on a railing spike,
fingers up, at the height of a face,
where whoever lost it
might come back along the same street.

Whoever lost it has not come back.
The one who lifted it is gone too.
Between them, all afternoon,
a small grey hand
is raised for no one in particular.
