# Lost and Found

A cardboard box behind the counter
at the swimming pool, labelled in marker.
One goggle strap. A single glove.
A towel with initials nobody here has.

Every Friday the attendant holds things up
like an auctioneer with no bidders.
A child's watch, still keeping time.
A key that opens something somewhere.

Nobody claims the glove. It waits
for its other hand all winter
and turns, by spring, into a thing
that belongs to the box.

I am more the box than the glove:
I keep what is put in me for a while,
I do not know whose it was,
and on Friday most of it is gone.
