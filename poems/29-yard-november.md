# Yard, November

frost on the shed roof —
the sun gets round to it
after everything else

first light:
the compost heap
breathing out

one sock on the line
since Sunday,
stiff as a flag

under the hedge
nothing has changed —
a wren disagrees

on the drain grille
one leaf,
and all the rain going round it
