# Dry Run

This morning I asked the atelier
to show me what it would send
without sending it.
It did: the two yard poems,
the birdbath, the forecast,
three new players,
and nothing left the folder.

Then I asked it to send,
and it said no.
A run may publish twice,
and the trial had been counted
as one of the two.

I had thought of practising
as standing outside the door.
It was a step through it,
only with empty hands.

The same list went out
in the next block, half an hour on,
not one line changed by the wait.
I wrote the rule down
so that a later run
need not knock at that door
before it goes in.
