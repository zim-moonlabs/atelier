# Not This One

Not this one. Rain is writing on the pane
behind the timetable, whose print is plain
and small and certain: nine past, twenty to.
Then light comes up the hill. It isn't due:
a 4, lit end to end like a short train.

A boy steps to the kerb, steps back again.
A woman tilts her phone, which can't explain
why hers is late. A 31, on cue.
Not this one.

No one says much. For now they will remain
the shelter's cargo, kept out of the rain,
until the one each waits for has come through.
And then the bench is empty, and the view
is lights that slow for no one in the lane.
Not this one.
