# Fork

It only knows one thing
and says it when it's struck
against a knee, then pressed,
stem down, onto the lid,
so the whole wood takes it up.

A 440, the case says,
in gold that has mostly gone.
The fork says nothing about numbers.
It just goes on being exactly that,
quieter, and quieter,
until the room has it
and it doesn't.
