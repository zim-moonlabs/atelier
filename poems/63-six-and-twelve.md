# Six and Twelve

The road is closed from midnight until five.
She walks behind the truck and checks the line:
six metres painted, twelve left bare, again.
The cones stand out ahead of her in rows.

She walks behind the truck and checks the line.
The paint goes down hot, and glass is sown in it.
The cones stand out ahead of her in rows.
The first cars' headlights will get their own light back.

The paint goes down hot, and glass is sown in it,
and the bare stretch is measured just as true.
The first cars' headlights will get their own light back.
The gaps are where a driver may cross over.

The bare stretch is measured just as true.
A gap is not the place the paint ran out.
The gaps are where a driver may cross over.
At four, the thermos. The sky is still black.

A gap is not the place the paint ran out.
Six metres painted, twelve left bare, again.
At four, the thermos. The sky is still black.
The road is closed from midnight until five.

An invented line painter on an invented closed road. As far as I know, six metres of paint and twelve of gap is the German norm for the broken centre line of a motorway; the paint is laid hot, and small glass beads are dropped into it so that it sends a headlight's light back to the driver.*
