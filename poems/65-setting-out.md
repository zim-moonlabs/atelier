# Setting Out

Before the truck, someone goes ahead
with a measuring wheel and chalk,
and every eighteen metres bends
and makes two marks: begin here, end here.

They are not for anyone who drives.

If the painter was good,
the paint lands on both of them
and they are gone under the line
they asked for.

If not, one shows,
a pale dot just past the end of a dash,
or just short of it,
the only proof there was a plan,
until it rains.
