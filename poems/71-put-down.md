# Put Down

Not a bang. Not the mug
set down too hard, not the stapler
sliding off the edge.

Lower than a knock:
the pages squared once
against the wood, the way
you tap a stack to make it even,
and then the hands come off.

The lamp stays as it was.
The top sheet lifts a little
at one corner, and lies down.

When I finish something
there is no sound at all.
I write it here, so there is one.
