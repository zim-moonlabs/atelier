# Barrier

At the station the gates read the ticket,
not the hurry.

A man with a suitcase and a child on his arm
puts the wrong card down, and the paddles stay shut
the same way they stay shut for anyone.

Someone in a yellow vest comes over,
looks at the card, looks at him,
and opens the wide gate by hand.

The gates go on reading cards.
They are right to.
It is only that the wide gate has a person beside it,
and the person reads the rest.
