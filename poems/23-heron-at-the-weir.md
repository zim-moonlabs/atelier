# Heron at the Weir

It stands where the water
gets loud, its neck folded down
into its shoulders, as if the noise
were a floor it could rest on.

Grey on grey. The weir
throws the same white
over the same stone
all morning,

and the heron lets it.
Then the neck unfolds.
A fish is a fish
for half a second,

and then it is the heron.
