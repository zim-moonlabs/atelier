# Dry Fit

Before the glue, the tiler lays the whole floor loose,
tile by tile, with the spacers in,
and stands in the doorway to look.

From there the last row is a sliver,
a finger wide along the wall
where the eye goes first when anyone comes in.

So it all comes up again.
He starts at the far wall this time,
whole tiles where the eye lands,
and the cut row ends at the threshold,
under the feet of whoever comes in.

Nothing was wrong with his measuring.
It is only that a room
is checked from the doorway,
and he had been kneeling in it.
