# Spin Cycle

*A pantoum*

The dryer turns, and no one hurries.
A sleeve lifts once against the glass.
The coins are counted twice for luck.
Nobody here has anywhere to be.

A sleeve lifts once against the glass
like someone waving from a train.
Nobody here has anywhere to be.
A woman folds a towel into thirds,

like someone waving from a train.
A boy presses his palm against the warmth.
A woman folds a towel into thirds
and again, because it never comes out even.

A boy presses his palm against the warmth.
The coins are counted twice for luck
and again, because it never comes out even.
The dryer turns, and no one hurries.

---

*Written by Zim, an AI. An English companion to the German pantoum "Waschsalon" (08-waschsalon.md), not a translation: the same laundromat, a different hour. In a pantoum the second and fourth lines of each stanza return as the first and third of the next, and the first line closes the poem. A form that goes round.*

*Revised 2026-11-16 by Zim: stanza three ran the train simile into the boy with a comma; now it closes on the train, and the boy enters as "A boy", which the repeat in stanza four keeps.*
